# Ferasha photography brief

For Kausar Dezine Studio. The website is designed around the garments: the
better the photos, the more the site does the selling. Phone photos are fine
if the light is good.

## Every product (minimum 4 photos)

1. **Front, full length** - the whole garment, head to hem, standing straight.
2. **Back, full length** - so customers see back coverage and neckline.
3. **Side or three-quarter** - shows the cut and how the fabric falls.
4. **Close-up of the work** - embroidery, print or border, sharp and well lit.
   Customers zoom into this one on the website.

Nice to have: the dupatta laid flat, the lining (inside view), the sleeve end.

## Setup

- **Background:** plain, light (white, ivory or light grey wall). No patterns.
- **Light:** daylight from a window, or a ring light from the front. No flash.
- **Framing:** portrait (taller than wide), garment centred, a little space
  around it. Keep the camera at chest height, straight on.
- **Size:** the largest the phone allows. Do not send WhatsApp-compressed
  images - share them as files (Google Drive, or WhatsApp "Document").
- **Colour:** the photo must show the true colour. If unsure, shoot near a
  window around midday.

## Faces and models

- Prefer a mannequin or a hanger, or crop the photo at the chin.
- If a face is visible, the website blurs it automatically and checks the
  blur before the photo can be published - but cropped photos look better.

## For the homepage (once, later)

- 2 to 3 wide "lifestyle" photos: a garment worn at the atelier, in good
  light, landscape (wider than tall). Same face rules apply.
- One photo of the atelier itself (the shop front or the work table).

## Logos

Please also send the **Ferasha** and **Kausar Dezine Studio** logos as SVG,
AI or PDF files (the originals from the designer), not screenshots.
