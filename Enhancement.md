# Enhancements

A running log of what has changed on Ferasha, why, and what is next. Updated
with every change: newest first, each entry with its commit, so the owner and
anyone joining can see the state of the shop at a glance.

Every frontend change ships with its Storybook story (`frontend/stories`),
run before it is committed. If a story could not be run, the entry says so.

Photos with faces are blurred and approved automatically. On approval the shop makes its own copy with the camera details and location stripped, and only that copy is ever public; who approved it and when is recorded. Approved photos then appear in the product's image picker. Still to do: uploading straight from a product's page, and attaching a photo to a product before its check finishes. | this commit |
